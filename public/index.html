Performance mode

The game lowers its own resolution a little when frames run slow and raises it again when they recover. Performance mode is for when it still stutters: it drops the resolution, turns off shadows and thins out zombies and other players. Frame rate is a limit: the game never runs faster than your screen refreshes. Resolution, shadows and zombie count change straight away; surface detail and reflections change the next time you open the game.
